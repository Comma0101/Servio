# Product Context: Servio Voice AI Agent

## The Problem

Businesses, particularly in the service industry like restaurants, face challenges with managing high volumes of customer interactions, especially phone orders. This can lead to missed calls, long wait times, and inconsistent customer service, resulting in lost revenue and poor customer experience. Human agents are costly and can only handle one call at a time.

## The Solution: Servio

Servio is a voice AI agent designed to automate these interactions. It provides a scalable, efficient, and natural-feeling conversational experience for customers. By handling routine tasks like taking orders, answering frequently asked questions, and providing information, Servio frees up human staff to focus on higher-value activities.

## Target Use Case: Restaurant Order-Taking

The primary application for Servio is automating phone orders for restaurants. The system is designed to:

- **Understand Natural Language:** Customers can speak naturally, and the NLU processor will interpret their requests, identifying items, quantities, and special instructions.
- **Access Dynamic Menus:** The agent can access restaurant-specific menus, likely from a database or a service like `thirty_nine_miles`, to provide accurate information and handle orders correctly.
- **Handle Complex Dialogues:** The system can manage multi-turn conversations, clarify ambiguities, and confirm orders before finalizing them.
- **Provide a Human-like Experience:** By using high-quality TTS and STT services and supporting barge-in, the agent aims to provide a conversational experience that is as natural as speaking to a human.

## User Experience Goals

- **Efficiency:** Customers should be able to place their orders quickly and without friction.
- **Accuracy:** The agent must accurately capture the customer's order to ensure satisfaction.
- **Natural Interaction:** The conversation should feel natural and not robotic. Customers should not have to adapt their speech to the machine.
- **Reliability:** The service must be available 24/7 to capture all potential orders.
